Sozdil — Wordle in the Kazakh language

Sozdil (Kazakh: Сөзділ) is a free daily word game in the Kazakh language — the Kazakh version of Wordle. Every day at 00:00 Kazakhstan time three new words come out: 4, 5 and 6 letters long. You have 6 tries for each word. No sign-up, no ads; it runs in any browser on phone or desktop.

Play Sozdil →

The words are Kazakh and the game interface is in Kazakh.

How to play

  1. Pick a mode: 4, 5 or 6 letters.
  2. Type any Kazakh word and press «Енгізу» (Enter).
  3. The tile colours show which letters you got.
  4. Find the word in 6 tries and share your result.

What the colours mean

  • Solid green — the letter is in the word and in the right spot.
  • Light green with a green border — the letter is in the word but in a different spot.
  • Grey — the letter is not in the word.

FAQ

What is Sozdil?

Sozdil (Сөзділ) is Wordle in the Kazakh language: every day you guess a hidden Kazakh word of 4, 5 or 6 letters in 6 tries. It is free and needs no sign-up.

Do I need to know Kazakh?

The hidden words are always Kazakh, so it works best for Kazakh speakers and learners. The rules are the same as in Wordle.

When does a new word come out?

Every day at 00:00 Kazakhstan time. Everyone gets the same word, so you can compare results with friends.

Is there an app?

You can play in any browser. You can also add it to your home screen as a PWA, or install the Android APK.

How do I type Kazakh letters?

The game has a built-in Kazakh keyboard. On a computer any layout works, and the letters Ә, І, Ң, Ғ, Ү, Ұ, Қ, Ө, Һ are on the number keys 1–9.
